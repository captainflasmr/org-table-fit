Scrolling keeps the cursor at least 10 visual lines from the window's top and bottom edges (Emacs =scroll-margin=), reaching the edges only at the buffer's own start/end; =C-l= (recenter) now respects the same margin — middle puts point at the centre, top on the margin line, bottom the same distance from the bottom.

Matching delimiters: the whole balanced =() [] {}= block at point is drawn bold (Emacs =show-paren-mode=, whole block not just the parens), and =C-M-f= / =C-M-b= step across one sexp, =C-M-SPC= marks it.
